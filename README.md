# Album 84 — Archivists and the Infinite Aisle | SCP 2801–3400 Intercession | KJC-DNN

## Lord of the Seas — The Second Watch

Seven-song worship cycle under Christ as Lord of the seas and of history. Ranges SCP 2801–3400 are named for intercession only. Official wiki titles are distinguished from prayer overlays (VOC greed, Leviathan, Tiamat/Marduk refused, Inanna-letter, Chronos, Aristocrats, Archivists, 3008 aisle, 3301 game). Core 7 + Expanded 7 + New 7 including Holy Jest. Trinity is never mocked. Humour strikes the anomaly only. No ritual, market, or weapons instruction. Jesus is Saviour and Victorious.

The Squid, the Sailor, and the Lord of the Deep (2846 / 2826 overlay)

Red Reality, Endless Aisle, Reliquary of Lies (3001–3100)

Sphere of Ice, Aristocrats of the Dark, and the Rain That Would Not Stop (3201–3300)
Archivists, Infinite Aisle, and the Last Lighthouse (3301–3400 + sweep 2800–3400)
